You belong here

A community that doesn't have it all together

Honestly? We're students who are still figuring life out. We ask big questions together, read the Bible and talk about what it actually means, and try to love the people around us the way we believe Jesus loves us.

Whether you grew up in church, walked away from it, or have never opened a Bible in your life, there's a place for you here. Come as you are — curious, doubtful, exhausted, hopeful. We'd love to get to know you.

What we do

Four things we're always up to

If you spent a semester with us, here's what you'd find us doing on campus.

Witnessing communities

Small communities where it's safe to be known, ask questions, and discover what following Jesus looks like together.

Bible study

Student-led, conversational, and surprisingly fun. No experience needed — just curiosity and a willingness to dig in.

Evangelism

We love telling friends about the real hope we've found in Jesus — through honest conversations, not pressure.

Leadership development

We help students grow as leaders on campus today — and as leaders in their workplaces, churches, and communities tomorrow.

College is one of the most formative seasons of life. We want to spend it building something that lasts.

Thursday nights

What to expect at large group

Once a week the whole chapter gets together for worship, a down-to-earth talk from the Bible, and time to actually hang out. It's relaxed, it's welcoming, and yes — there's usually food.

You don't have to believe anything in particular to come. Bring a friend, sit in the back, leave whenever you need to. We're just glad you're here.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to be a Christian to come?
Not at all. Plenty of people who come to InterVarsity are exploring, doubting, or just curious. You're welcome exactly as you are — no pressure, no weird vibes.
When and where do you meet?
Our large group meets Thursdays at 7:00 PM in Jamrich Hall, Room 1100. Small groups meet throughout the week in the dorms and around campus. (Times shown are placeholders for this mock-up.)
How do I join a small group?
Easy — fill out the form below and tell us a bit about your schedule. We'll connect you with a group and a leader who'd love to have you.
What does it cost?
Coming to large group and small groups is completely free. Retreats and conferences have a cost, but we keep them affordable and scholarships are always available — money should never keep you from coming.
I'm a parent or faculty member. How can I help?
Thank you! You can pray for the chapter, partner financially, or get in touch about faculty ministry. Reach out using the form below and we'll point you in the right direction.
